About Publications

I am a 1st year PhD student in the Department of Computer and Information Science at the University of Pennsylvania, where I am a member of the Distributed Systems Laboratory and am fortunate to be advised by Professor Linh Thi Xuan Phan. Previously, I worked closely with Professor Dimitrios Papadopoulos. I received my MPhil from the HKUST and my bachelor's degree from Xidian University.

My current research focuses on: (1) developing energy-efficient systems for AI and LLM datacenters, and (2) designing efficient agentic LLM systems. That said, my interests span computer systems broadly. Previously, I worked on learned systems, applying ML to resource allocation [MPORA@ECRTS'26] and network inspection [ENIDrift@ACSAC'22], as well as on trusted computation for edge AI [SOTER@USENIX ATC'22] and databases [Obliviator@USENIX-Sec'25, DISCO*@SOSP'26].

I enjoy building efficient systems and exploring new research ideas. If you share similar interests, I'd be happy to chat about related topics.
